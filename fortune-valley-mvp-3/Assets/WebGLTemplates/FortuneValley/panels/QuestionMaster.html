How QuestionMaster works

Answer correctly to earn cash. Each correct answer in a row pays more than the last. A wrong answer or timeout resets your streak.

  • Timer. You have 15 seconds per question.
  • Streak. Rewards grow with every correct answer in a row.
  • Reset. A wrong answer or running out of time sends your streak back to zero.
  • Categories. Questions cover Investing, Credit, Insurance, Budget, and Taxes.
